## The meme

A pure, official-looking fake document — no mascot, no characters, nothing else in frame:

- **LEONIDA COUNTY — OFFICE OF THE SHERIFF**
- **NOTICE TO VACATE**
- Tenant: **GRAND THEFT AUTO V**
- Premises: Los Santos, San Andreas
- In occupancy since: September 17, 2013 — *"You have overstayed THIRTEEN YEARS."*
- **TOTAL DUE: $0.00**
- *"The new tenant, GRAND THEFT AUTO VI, has moved in and changed the locks. VACATE IMMEDIATELY."*
- Case No. VI-2026-1123, a red **EVICTED** stamp and a gold seal.

## Why this formula

The top of this bounty's board is held by clean fake documents (an eviction notice at #2, a fake newspaper obituary at #1). An earlier version of this entry dressed the same joke up with a mascot character serving the notice and scored far lower (rank 651, judge 821,290). This version copies the winning formula faithfully: the document *is* the joke — legible at timeline size, GTA 6-specific, and the "total due $0.00" punchline lands without any decoration.
